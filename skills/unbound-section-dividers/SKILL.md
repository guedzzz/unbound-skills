# Unbound section dividers

Unbound theme only. Shapes are locked: Wave, Soft wave, Slant, Arch, Arch offset, Paper cut, Triangle, Triangle offset, Arrow, Split, Book, Peaks, Steps, Corner cut, Rounded corner, Blob.

Paint with Use theme settings or Custom. Custom is Use surface, Color with opacity, or Gradient. Do not add a shape and do not bake a hex color.

Pilot is Hero Banner. Overlap into the next section is a separate toggle, default off. Spacer stays a space or a rule.
